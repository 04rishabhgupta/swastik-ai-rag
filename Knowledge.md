EMG / NCV — website-described purpose

NCS / EMG contraindications and precautions stated by site
The site lists the following as contraindications/concerns for certain studies:
Pacemaker / Swan-Ganz catheter
Wounds or infection at the test site
AV fistula at the test site
Ongoing anticoagulant therapy for needle EMG (examples given include warfarin/coumadin and heparin)
Active bleeding / bleeding disorders such as thrombocytopenia or hemophilia
Certain infection-susceptibility situations (site gives examples such as valvular heart disease, prosthetic valves, endocarditis)
These are site-published statements. A patient should be advised to discuss individual eligibility with the treating clinician rather than receiving a definitive safety clearance from the RAG assistant.
VEP — website-described purpose
VEP is described as testing the visual pathway from the retina to the occipital cortex and measuring conduction through the optic nerve, optic chiasm, optic radiations and occipital cortex.
Symptoms listed include:
Loss of vision, painful or painless
Double vision
Blurred vision
Flashing lights
Altered colour vision
BAEP / BAER — website-described purpose
BAEP/BAER is described as a hearing-related evoked potential that detects electrical activity in the cochlea and auditory pathways in the brain. The site says it may assist in assessment of hearing loss and may be used in young children, including those under 2 years, for hearing-related screening.
The site lists potential indications such as:
Hearing loss
Classification of conductive vs sensorineural deafness
Unilateral tinnitus
Poor word recognition relative to sensorineural hearing loss
Perceived sound distortion with otherwise normal peripheral hearing
Retrocochlear pathology, including acoustic neuroma / vestibular schwannoma
RNST / repetitive nerve stimulation — website-described purpose
RNS is described as an important confirmatory test for disorders of the nicotinic neuromuscular junction, particularly:
Myasthenia gravis
Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome
Botulism
Symptoms listed include:
Weakness and fatigability
Ptosis / drooping eyelids
Diplopia / double vision
Dysphagia / difficulty swallowing
Dysarthria / slurred speech
